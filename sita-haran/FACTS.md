# Facts and choices

The film follows the episode as most viewers know it: Valmiki's Ramayana (Aranya Kanda) plus details
from later retellings. Where the two differ, the film notes it here.

| On screen | Source / note |
|---|---|
| Rama, Sita and Lakshmana live in a hut at Panchavati, by the Godavari, during their exile | Valmiki, Aranya Kanda |
| "13 of 14 years": the abduction happens late in the exile, in its 13th year | Valmiki places it near the end of the 13th year; tellings vary slightly |
| Ravana, the ten-headed king of Lanka | Valmiki |
| The golden deer is Maricha in disguise, sent by Ravana; Sita asks for it and Rama chases it | Valmiki |
| Struck by Rama's arrow, the deer cries out in Rama's voice ("Ha Sita! Ha Lakshmana!") | Valmiki |
| Sita sends Lakshmana after Rama | Valmiki |
| **Lakshmana draws a protective line around the hut** (the Lakshmana Rekha) | **Not in Valmiki.** It comes from later retellings and is the version most people know; the film uses it because the story is about that line |
| Ravana arrives disguised as a wandering holy man asking for alms; Sita steps out | Valmiki (alms); stepping over the line belongs to the later tellings |
| Ravana carries her off through the sky in a chariot | Valmiki (an aerial chariot) |
| Jatayu, the old vulture, fights Ravana and falls, his wing cut | Valmiki |
| Sita drops her jewels, tied in her upper cloth, to monkeys on a hill (Rishyamuka) | Valmiki |
| She is held in a grove of ashoka trees (Ashoka Vatika) in Lanka | Valmiki |
| The map | Diagrammatic, no borders, **not to scale** (the real distance from Panchavati near Nashik to Lanka is well over a thousand kilometres) |
